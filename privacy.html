Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 27, 2026

Trading Bot Drive is a personal application operated by its owner to store market-data research reports in the owner's Google Drive account.

Google Drive access

The application requests Google Drive access to upload, read, organize, and delete research reports in a folder selected by the owner.

Data handling

Google OAuth credentials are stored privately on the owner's server. Research reports are stored in the owner's Google Drive account. Google user data is not sold or used for advertising.

Sharing and retention

The application is intended solely for personal use. The owner controls the reports stored in Google Drive and may delete them or revoke the application's access through Google Account settings.
